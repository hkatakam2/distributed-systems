# Mission: Backend Distributed Systems Engineer

## Why
Become the backend distributed systems engineer represented by `resume.tex`: someone who can design, build, debug, and defend production systems for high-throughput APIs, event-driven pipelines, real-time materialized views, cloud deployments, and AI infrastructure. The goal is transferable technical judgment, not memorization of specific tools.

## Success looks like
- Explain a production backend system from request path to storage, async processing, caching, observability, deployment, and failure recovery.
- Defend resume-style bullets with concrete design choices, tradeoffs, metrics, failure modes, and operational details.
- Recognize common industry patterns behind technologies such as Kafka, Flink, Redis, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, AWS, gRPC, and OpenTelemetry.
- Transfer the same principles to equivalent tools such as Pub/Sub, Pulsar, Beam, Spark Streaming, DynamoDB, Cassandra, ECS, Nomad, Datadog, or Honeycomb.
- Ramp quickly over four weeks with substantial cumulative lessons rather than small isolated tutorials.

## Constraints
- Current level: intermediate; complex material is welcome when explained clearly from first principles.
- Focus: technical skill and production judgment, not DSA, behavioral prep, or generic interview coaching.
- Lesson style: bigger cumulative lessons with big-picture framing, practical details, tradeoffs, and reflection questions.
- No homework required, but include retrieval and reflection prompts to test understanding.

## Out of scope
- Full DSA interview preparation.
- Behavioral storytelling practice except where needed to technically defend system decisions.
- Tool-only tutorials that do not explain the broader distributed systems problem being solved.
